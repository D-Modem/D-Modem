# V.250 6.4.3, 6.5.5 and 6.6.3: with +MR=1, +ER=1 and +DR=1, the
# modem reports +MCR, +MRR, +ER and +DR before CONNECT (and +ILRR
# with +ILRR=1 and a fixed +IPR); B, with them 0, reports none.
# The second call has no error control (+ER: NONE) and +MR=0
A ATE0X3+MS=122+MR=1+ER=1+DR=1+IPR=19200+ILRR=1\r
B ATE0+MS=122\r
B ATA\r
A ATD\r
until A CONNECT 30000
until B CONNECT 2000
state
hangup A
until B NO CARRIER 10000
run 500
state
A AT+ES=0,0,1+MR=0+IPR=0\r
until A OK 1000
B ATA\r
A ATD\r
until A CONNECT 30000
until B CONNECT 10000
state
hangup A
until B NO CARRIER 10000
run 500
state
