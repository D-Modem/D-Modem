# V.34: up to 33600 bit/s duplex on the telephone network

This page describes ITU-T Recommendation V.34 (02/98), "A modem
operating at data signalling rates of up to 33 600 bit/s for use on the
general switched telephone network and on leased point-to-point 2-wire
telephone-type circuits". It is our own description. It is not a copy of
the Recommendation. For the normative text, refer to the Recommendation
(`docs/specs/fetch.sh V.34`). For the signals, the start-up and the
timing, refer to [the technical details](v34-details.md).

## Summary

V.34 sends data at 2400 bit/s to 33600 bit/s, in steps of 2400 bit/s, in
the two directions at the same time.

- The modem selects its symbol rate (2400 to 3429 symbols each second)
  and its carrier from a probe of the line. Thus, it uses as much of the
  band of the line as it can.
- Shell mapping, a four-dimensional trellis code and precoding give a
  data rate near the limit of the line.
- The start-up has four phases:
  1. V.8.
  2. The line probe and the round-trip delay.
  3. The training of the equalizers and the echo cancellers.
  4. The selection of the rates.
- A control channel at 600 bit/s or 1200 bit/s carries the start-up
  information. A rate renegotiation changes the rate without a full
  retrain.
- An optional half-duplex mode (clause 12) sends fax pages at up to
  33600 bit/s, with a duplex control channel for the T.30 signals.

## Where V.34 is used

V.34 is the fastest analogue modulation of the telephone network. V.90
and V.92 use its start-up and its upstream. V.34 fax (T.30 Annex F) uses
the half-duplex mode. A V.8 exchange always comes before V.34.

## Related standards

- [V.8](v8.md): the exchange that selects V.34.
- [V.32 bis](v32bis.md): the fallback when the far modem does not have
  V.34.
- [V.90](v90.md), [V.91](v91.md) and [V.92](v92.md): they use the start-up, the control channel and
  the upstream of V.34.
- T.30 Annex F: fax with the half-duplex mode.

## V.34 in D-Modem

`dsp/dp_v34.cpp` has the V.34 data pump (`AT+MS=34`). `dsp/t30_v34.cpp`
uses the half-duplex mode for fax. The implementation is described in
[V.8 and V.34](../dsp/v34.md).
