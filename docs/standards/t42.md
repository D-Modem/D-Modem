# T.42: colour fax with JPEG

This page describes ITU-T Recommendation T.42 (07/2003), "Continuous-tone
colour representation method for facsimile". It is our own
description. It is not a copy of the Recommendation. For the normative
text, refer to the Recommendation (`docs/specs/fetch.sh T.42`). For the
colour space and its octets, refer to [the technical
details](t42-details.md).

## Summary

T.42 gives the colour space of colour and grey-scale fax pages.

- The basic colour space is CIELAB, with the CIE illuminant D50.
- The basic gamut is L* 0 to 100, a* -85 to 85 and b* -75 to 125. Each
  component is an 8-bit or 12-bit integer, with a range and an offset.
- A grey-scale page has only L*.
- Other gamuts and illuminants are options that the fax procedure must
  negotiate. T.42 also gives a YCC space (from sYCC, D65) for displays.
- T.4 Annex E codes the samples with JPEG (T.81) for colour fax. T.43
  and T.44 use the same colour space.

## Where T.42 is used

Colour fax machines and fax servers use T.42 with JPEG for colour and
grey-scale pages, mostly photographs.

## Related standards

- [T.4](t4.md): Annex E (JPEG colour fax) and the page sizes.
- [T.30](t30.md): negotiates JPEG and colour with DIS/DCS bits 68 to 73.
- [T.43](t43.md) and [T.44](t44.md): other colour codings in the same
  colour space.

## T.42 in D-Modem

`dsp/jpeg.cpp` converts sRGB to the T.42 CIELAB octets and back, and
codes the samples with baseline JPEG for T.4 Annex E.
