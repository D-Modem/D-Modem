# V.92: quick connect, modem-on-hold and PCM upstream

This page describes ITU-T Recommendation V.92 (11/2000), "Enhancements to
Recommendation V.90". It is our own description. It is not a copy of the
Recommendation. For the normative text, refer to the Recommendation
(`docs/specs/fetch.sh V.92`). For the signals, the start-up and the
timing, refer to [the technical details](v92-details.md).

## Summary

V.92 keeps the digital modem and the analogue modem of V.90, and adds
three functions:

- **Quick connect.** A short phase 1 and a short phase 2 make the
  start-up shorter. The analogue modem keeps the line data of the last
  call. The digital modem sends a known PCM sequence (ANSpcm) that lets
  the analogue modem check the line quickly.
- **Modem-on-hold.** When a second call comes in (call waiting), a modem
  can ask the far modem to wait. The user can answer the telephone. Then
  the data call continues, with no full start-up.
- **PCM upstream.** The analogue modem can also send PCM codes. The
  upstream rate is then 24000 bit/s to 48000 bit/s, in steps of
  8000/6 bit/s, in place of the 33600 bit/s maximum of V.34.

The downstream direction is the same as V.90: 28000 bit/s to
56000 bit/s.

## Where V.92 is used

V.92 is the last standard for dial-up modems. Most dial-up modems made
after 2000 have it. A modem that does not have V.92 connects with V.90 or
V.34.

## Related standards

- [V.90](v90.md): the downstream direction, phase 3 and phase 4.
- [V.34](v34.md): phase 2, and the upstream direction when PCM upstream
  is not used.
- [V.8](v8.md) and [V.8 bis](v8bis.md): phase 1. Quick connect makes
  them shorter.
- V.44: the data compression that V.92 calls normally use.
- G.711: the µ-law and A-law PCM codes.

## V.92 in D-Modem

D-Modem is the digital modem and the analogue modem of V.92. It does
quick connect, modem-on-hold and PCM upstream at the two ends
(`dsp/v92.cpp`, `dsp/v92a.cpp` and `dsp/dp_v34.cpp`). The implementation
is described in [V.92](../dsp/v92.md) and [V.90 and V.92: the analogue
modem](../dsp/v90a.md).
