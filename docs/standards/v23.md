# V.23: 1200/75 bit/s FSK

This page describes ITU-T Recommendation V.23 (11/1988), "600/1200-baud
modem standardized for use in the general switched telephone network".
It is our own description. It is not a copy of the Recommendation. For
the normative text, refer to the Recommendation (`docs/specs/fetch.sh
V.23`). For the frequencies, the levels and the timing, refer to
[the technical details](v23-details.md).

## Summary

V.23 sends data at 600 baud or 1200 baud on a forward channel. It can
also have a backward channel of 75 baud in the other direction.

V.23 uses frequency shift keying (FSK), as [V.21](v21.md) does. The
modem sends one tone for a ONE bit and a different tone for a ZERO bit.

- **The forward channel** carries the data. Mode 1 operates at 600 baud
  or less. Mode 2 operates at 1200 baud or less.
- **The backward channel** is optional. It operates at 75 baud or less.
  It is for error control, or for a slow return path.

## Where V.23 is used

- **Videotex.** Minitel in France and Prestel in the United Kingdom used
  V.23. The service sent pages at 1200 bit/s. The terminal sent the keys
  at 75 bit/s.
- **Caller ID and SMS on fixed lines.** Some networks send the caller
  number, and short messages, in the forward channel of V.23.
- **Text telephones.** V.18 has a V.23 mode for Minitel terminals.

## How a V.23 call operates

1. The call modem dials. The answer modem answers with the answer tone of
   [V.25](v25.md).
2. The answer modem sends ONE bits (a continuous mark tone) on the forward
   channel.
3. The call modem detects the carrier. It sends ONE bits on the backward
   channel.
4. The two modems send data: 1200 bit/s from the answer modem, 75 bit/s
   from the call modem.

V.23 has no training, no scrambler and no equalizer.

## Related standards

- [V.21](v21.md): the 300 bit/s FSK modem. It uses the same detector
  levels.
- [V.25](v25.md): the answer tone before a V.23 call.
- V.18: text telephones, with a V.23 mode.
- Bell 202: the 1200 bit/s FSK modem of North America. It uses different
  frequencies.

## V.23 in D-Modem

`dsp/dp_fsk.cpp` has the V.23 pump (`AT+MS=V23C`), with mode 2 and the
backward channel. The answer modem sends at 1200 bit/s. D-Modem does not
have mode 1 (600 baud). The fixed-line SMS pump (`dsp/dp_sms.cpp`) and
the text telephone modes (`dsp/dp_text.cpp`) use the same FSK code.
