# V.70: digital simultaneous voice and data

This page describes ITU-T Recommendation V.70 (08/96), "Procedures for
the simultaneous transmission of data and digitally encoded voice
signals over the GSTN, or over 2-wire leased point-to-point telephone
type circuits". It is our own description. It is not a copy of the
Recommendation. For the normative text, refer to the Recommendation
(`docs/specs/fetch.sh V.70`). For the parts, the roles and the
procedures, refer to [the technical details](v70-details.md).

## Summary

V.70 (DSVD) lets two persons talk and send data on one telephone call.
The modem codes the voice digitally and sends it beside the data.

- V.8 bis selects the DSVD mode, at the start of the call or during a
  voice call. It also gives the roles: the V.8 bis initiator is the V.70
  initiator.
- The modem is V.34 or V.32 bis. Its synchronous bit stream carries one
  HDLC link.
- V.76 divides the link into data link connections (DLCs). A data DLC
  uses error recovery (ERM). A voice DLC uses UI frames (UNERM).
- V.75 opens and closes each DLC with H.245 messages in the SABME, UA,
  DM and DISC frames.
- The voice coder is G.729 Annex A: 8 kbit/s, a 10-octet block each
  10 ms. G.729 Annex B silence compression is optional.

## Where V.70 is used

DSVD modems of the late 1990s used V.70, for example for games and for
help desks. A PBX can also use it.

## Related standards

- [V.8 bis](v8bis.md): the selection of the DSVD mode.
- [V.75](v75.md): the control of the DLCs.
- [V.76](v76.md): the multiplexer.
- G.729 Annex A: the voice coder.
- H.245: the control messages.
- [V.34](v34.md) and [V.32 bis](v32bis.md): the modulations.
