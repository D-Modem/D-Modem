# V.91: PCM between two digital modems

This page describes ITU-T Recommendation V.91 (05/99), "A digital modem
operating at data signalling rates of up to 64 000 bit/s for use on a
4-wire circuit switched connection and on leased point-to-point 4-wire
digital circuits". It is our own description. It is not a copy of the
Recommendation. For the normative text, refer to the Recommendation
(`docs/specs/fetch.sh V.91`). For the signals, the start-up and the
timing, refer to [the technical details](v91-details.md).

## Summary

V.91 is for a call where the two modems are on digital connections. No
analogue line is between them. Thus, the two directions can send the
PCM codes of G.711 directly, as the downstream of V.90 does.

- The data rate is 28000 bit/s to 64000 bit/s, in steps of 8000/6 bit/s,
  in each direction.
- The encoder is the encoder of the V.90 digital modem, with all six
  sign bits for data (no spectral shaping).
- The two modems use the same start-up. Each modem sends INFO, then a
  digital impairment learning (DIL) sequence, then CP with the
  constellations that it can receive.
- A transparent mode sends the data bits as PCM codes with no change.
  Thus, it uses all of the 64000 bit/s of an unrestricted channel.
- An optional control channel of 125 bit/s detects digital slips and
  carries a cleardown or a retrain request.

## Where V.91 is used

V.91 connects two digital modems through a digital telephone network.
An example is a call from one D-Modem to a different D-Modem through
SIP or a telephone system. V.91 also operates on a 4-wire digital leased
circuit, with no V.8 before it. If V.91 does not start, the modems use
V.34.

## Related standards

- [V.8](v8.md): the exchange that selects V.91 on a switched call.
- [V.90](v90.md): the encoder, the DIL and the CP of V.91 come from
  V.90.
- [V.34](v34.md): the fallback, and the CRC of the control channel.
- G.711: the µ-law and A-law PCM codes.

## V.91 in D-Modem

`dsp/v91.cpp` has the V.91 modem. The V.34 pump (`dsp/dp_v34.cpp`) starts
it after V.8, or at once on a leased circuit (`AT&L2+MS=V91`). The
implementation is described in [V.91: digital to digital](../dsp/v91.md).
