# V.32: 9600 bit/s duplex on the telephone network

This page describes ITU-T Recommendation V.32 (03/93), "A family of
2-wire, duplex modems operating at data signalling rates of up to 9600
bit/s for use on the general switched telephone network and on leased
telephone-type circuits". It is our own description. It is not a copy of
the Recommendation. For the normative text, refer to the Recommendation
(`docs/specs/fetch.sh V.32`). For the signal, the start-up and the rate
signals, refer to [the technical details](v32-details.md).

## Summary

V.32 sends data at 9600 bit/s or 4800 bit/s in the two directions at the
same time, on one pair of wires.

- The two modems use the same band: an 1800 Hz carrier at 2400 symbols
  each second. An echo canceller in each modem removes its own signal
  from the received signal.
- At 4800 bit/s, each symbol carries two bits, on four points.
- At 9600 bit/s, each symbol carries four bits. The modem can send 16
  points without a code, or 32 points with a trellis code. The trellis
  code gives a better error rate on a noisy line. A 9600 bit/s modem must
  also operate without the trellis code.
- A scrambler makes the data random. The caller and the answerer use
  different scramblers.
- The start-up measures the round-trip delay, trains the echo cancellers
  and the equalizers, and selects the rate with rate signals.
- Each modem can start a retrain during the data.

## Where V.32 is used

V.32 was the first duplex 9600 bit/s modem for the switched network.
[V.32 bis](v32bis.md) adds faster rates and a rate change without a
retrain. A modern modem uses V.32 or V.32 bis after V.8, when the far
modem does not have V.34.

## Related standards

- [V.25](v25.md): the answer tone before the start-up.
- [V.32 bis](v32bis.md): 14400 bit/s on the same start-up.
- [V.22 bis](v22bis.md): Annex A gives an automode start-up that finds
  V.22 bis and V.22 modems.
- [V.26 ter](v26ter.md): the 2400 bit/s modem with echo cancellation.
  Appendix I tells how a modem can operate with the two.
- V.14 and V.42: the asynchronous modes.
- V.54: the test loops.

## V.32 in D-Modem

`dsp/dp_v32.cpp` has the V.32 data pump. `AT+MS=32` selects V.32 alone,
at 4800 bit/s and 9600 bit/s. `AT+MS=132` selects V.32 bis. The pump
also starts after V.8 when the far modem does not have V.34. It falls
back to V.22 bis, V.21 or V.23 when the far modem does not have V.32.
