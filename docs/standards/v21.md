# V.21: 300 bit/s duplex FSK

This page describes ITU-T Recommendation V.21 (11/1988), "300 bits per
second duplex modem standardized for use in the general switched
telephone network". It is our own description. It is not a copy of the
Recommendation. For the normative text, refer to the Recommendation
(`docs/specs/fetch.sh V.21`). For the frequencies, the levels and the
timing, refer to [the technical details](v21-details.md).

## Summary

V.21 is one of the first modem standards. It sends data at 300 bit/s or
less, in the two directions at the same time (duplex), on a usual
telephone line.

V.21 uses frequency shift keying (FSK). The modem sends one tone for a
ONE bit and a different tone for a ZERO bit. The band of the telephone
line has two channels:

- **Channel 1** (the low channel, near 1080 Hz). The call modem sends on
  it.
- **Channel 2** (the high channel, near 1750 Hz). The answer modem sends
  on it.

Because each modem sends on a different channel, the two modems can send
at the same time.

## Why V.21 is still important

Modern modems do not use V.21 for data. But V.21 is in all modern calls:

- V.8 sends its menus (CI, CM, JM and CJ) on the two channels of V.21.
- A Group 3 fax call (T.30) sends its control frames (HDLC) on channel 2
  of V.21.
- Text telephones (V.18) can use V.21.

Thus, a modem that does V.34, V.90, V.92 or fax must also have a V.21
transmitter and receiver.

## How a V.21 call operates

1. The call modem dials. The answer modem answers with the answer tone of
   V.25.
2. The answer modem sends ONE bits (a continuous mark tone) on channel 2.
3. The call modem detects the carrier. It sends ONE bits on channel 1.
4. The two modems send data. Each modem sends asynchronous characters or
   synchronous bits.

V.21 has no training, no scrambler and no equalizer. Thus, the start of a
call is fast and simple.

## Related standards

- [V.25](v25.md): the answer tone before a V.21 call.
- [V.8](v8.md): the start-up that uses the V.21 channels.
- [T.30](https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC-T.30): fax, which uses channel 2.
- Bell 103: the 300 bit/s modem of North America. It is almost the same
  as V.21, but it uses different frequencies.

## V.21 in D-Modem

`dsp/dp_fsk.cpp` has the V.21 pump (`AT+MS=V21`). The same FSK code is
also the V.21 part of V.8, of fax, of caller ID and of the text telephone
modes. If a far modem cannot do V.8, D-Modem tries V.21 after V.32bis and
V.22bis.
