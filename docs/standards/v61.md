# V.61: analogue simultaneous voice and data

This page describes ITU-T Recommendation V.61 (08/96), "A simultaneous
voice plus data modem, operating at a voice plus data signalling rate
of 4800 bit/s, with optional automatic switching to data-only
signalling rates of up to 14 400 bit/s, for use on the general switched
telephone network and on leased point-to-point 2-wire telephone-type
circuits". It is our own description. It is not a copy of the
Recommendation. For the normative text, refer to the Recommendation
(`docs/specs/fetch.sh V.61`). For the signals, the frames and the
procedures, refer to [the technical details](v61-details.md).

## Summary

V.61 sends speech and data on one telephone line at the same time. The
name of this mode is ASVD (analogue simultaneous voice and data).

- The modem sends 3000 symbols per second on an 1800 Hz carrier, or
  2800 symbols per second on a 1680 Hz carrier. The two directions use
  the same band. An echo canceller separates them.
- With speech, the data rate is 4800 bit/s on 4 points. The modem adds
  the speech to each data point, in the quadrant of that point. The
  receiver finds the quadrant, then takes the remainder as the speech.
- Without speech, the data rate is 4800, 7200, 9600, 12000 or 14400
  bit/s on uncoded points (4 to 64 points).
- The data is in frames of 70 symbols. Each frame has a control segment
  that tells how the next frame is sent: with speech or data only, and
  the gain of the speech.
- A modem can send data-only frames while its speech is silent. Then the
  data rate increases.
- The start-up measures the round trip delay, trains the echo
  cancellers and the equalizers, and selects the rate with rate
  signals. A retrain does the start-up again from the delay
  measurement. A rate renegotiation changes the rate without a retrain.

## Where V.61 is used

V.61 was a standard for voice and data modems before
[V.34](v34.md) and [V.70](v70.md) (DSVD). V.8 bis selects it at the
start of a call. Few modems have it.

## Related standards

- [V.8 bis](v8bis.md): selects ASVD at the start of the call.
- [V.25](v25.md): the answer tone before the start-up when V.8 bis is
  not used.
- [V.32 bis](v32bis.md): the same rate words, the same preamble and the
  same rate renegotiation.
- [V.42](v42.md) and V.14: the error correction and the
  asynchronous conversion on the data.
- [V.54](v54.md): the test loops 2 and 3.
- [V.70](v70.md): digital simultaneous voice and data (DSVD), the
  other voice and data mode.

## V.61 in D-Modem

`dsp/dp_v61.cpp` has the V.61 data pump (`AT+MS=61`, or `AT+ASVD=1`
with V.8 bis). The speech comes from the voice path of the modem (a
handset, or `dmodemd --dsvd-audio`). Without a voice path, every frame
is data only. `ATO1` starts a retrain, and `ATO3` starts a rate
renegotiation.
