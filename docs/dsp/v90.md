# V.90: the digital modem

V.90 is the "56k" modem standard. A V.90 call has two different ends
(refer to [the data pumps](README.md#the-two-ends-of-a-56k-call)). This
page is about D-Modem as the **digital modem**. This is the end on a fully
digital telephone connection. In the 1990s, the equipment of an internet
provider had this role. D-Modem has this role on a SIP call or on the
channel of a telephone system. Thus, an old modem that dials in can connect
at a maximum of 56000 bit/s.

The code is `dsp/v90.cpp`, on the V.34 pump. For how the modem does each
phase of the start-up, and how it controls echo and the unusual behaviour
of other modems, refer to [the technical details](v90-details.md).

## What is necessary

The call must be **G.711 on the full path** (plain digital telephone
audio, µ-law or A-law). No item on the path can convert or encode the
audio again. This makes 56k possible. D-Modem sends exact digital values,
and the telephone network changes them into the exact voltages that the
modem at the other end measures.

## The sequence of a call

1. The modems agree on V.90 during V.8 (refer to [V.34](v34.md)).
2. They measure the line in the two directions, as V.34 does.
3. The digital modem sends a **digital impairment learning** sequence.
   The analogue modem uses it to find what the telephone network does to
   the digital values. Examples are volume changes ("pads"), "robbed bits"
   on some American trunks, and other changes.
4. The analogue modem selects the set of values that are not changed on
   the path, and the speed.
5. The data moves: a maximum of 56000 bit/s down (digital to analogue)
   and a maximum of 33600 bit/s up (analogue to digital, with V.34).

## Results

In the tests, real modems from other companies connect to D-Modem at
46666 to 54666 bit/s down. The speed changes with the modem (refer to
[Interop](../interop.md)). The original library of Smart Link connects at
54666 down and 31200 up on a simulated line.

Refer also to [V.90 analogue modem](v90a.md) (the other end), [V.92](v92.md)
(the next standard) and [V.91](v91.md) (two digital modems).
